Recommended path

  • v1 · built
    Block Watch pages for Petworth and Tenleytown

    Nightly ingest, Astro pages, RSS, Cloudflare Worker routing, GitHub Action. Ready to deploy once the domains are on Cloudflare.

  • Month 1–2
    Weekly digest email, plus Adams Morgan and Friendship Heights

    The email is the retention loop and the sponsor inventory. Friendship Heights needs its own boundary, because DC's "Tenleytown" polygon includes it.

  • Month 3–4
    Block and address pages

    Original 1900s building permits next to today's permits and 311. Evergreen search traffic and a realtor sponsorship slot.

  • Spring
    L'Enfant Transfer Coach

    Exits, escalators and live arrivals for the five-line transfer, ahead of tourist season. Affiliate links only on lenfantplaza.net.

Money, honestly

  • Model: one exclusive "presented by" sponsor per neighborhood digest, plus a few small slots. No display ads, no paid placement in the data, no lead selling.
  • Benchmarks found: newsletters under 5K subscribers sell placements for $50–250. One solo local newsletter reports about $6K a month from 12 sponsors on 2–3K subscribers. Display ads at this scale earn $25–100 a month.
  • Estimate at month 18: $0–300 a month conservative, $600–1,200 base, $2–4K upside. Costs are about $80–500 a year.
  • Decision point at month 4: keep going at 300+ subscribers in a neighborhood or growing search traffic; otherwise pivot to block pages only, or lease the domains.

Guardrails already in the code

  • Permit owner and applicant names are never requested from DC's servers.
  • 311 locations are coarsened to the hundred block; crime is fetched only as counts by offense.
  • No percentages on small counts, so 49 vs. 40 crime reports doesn't read as a spike.
  • Every page says it is independent and shows when its data was pulled. A warning appears if the data is more than 3 days old.
