About this demo

The idea
A chat box for visitors at L'Enfant Plaza that answers combined questions ("3 hours, a stroller, which museum and how?") using only a curated, regularly refreshed set of facts, cites each one with its as-of time, and refuses anything outside them.
What's real here
The conversation is scripted, but its facts are real where marked: Capital Bikeshare dock counts (F9) were pulled live at build time; station levels, entrances and elevators (F1–F3) come from WMATA's station page; the SW Shuttle schedule (F6) from wharfdc.com; Air and Space hours (F7) from si.edu as shown in search results (si.edu blocked our direct fetch). The shuttle answer is computed for the build date.
What's mocked
No language model runs here: the answers were written by hand to show the intended behavior. Elevator status (F4) and train times (F5) are sample. The typing box only keyword-matches the fact table. The walk time (F8) is a map-distance estimate.
To build it for real
Grounding design: a cron Worker refreshes a small fact store (D1 rows with id, text, source URL, fetched-at, type) from WMATA, NPS, NWS, GBFS, the shuttle page and per-museum si.edu scrapes. For each question, retrieval (keyword plus Vectorize) picks at most about 8 rows; the model (Workers AI or the Claude API) gets only those rows and a system prompt requiring a citation id on every sentence, "I don't know that; check X" when no row answers it, and no restaurant, medical or safety advice. A post-check drops any answer whose citations don't exist or whose numbers don't appear in the cited rows. Log questions that got refusals and add facts for the common ones. Rate-limit per IP to cap inference cost, and keep commerce on the .net domain.
