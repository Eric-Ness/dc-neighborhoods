About this demo

The idea
An auto-updating housing data page per neighborhood with one real-estate agent or team as its exclusive 12-month sponsor. The data stays neutral and public-record; the agent buys brand association, not leads.
What's real here
All prices, counts and charts. This run pulled 370 Petworth house and condo sales that OTR codes as "Market Sale" over 24 months from the Integrated Tax System Public Extract, building area and bedrooms from CAMA Residential, and 31 candidate permits from DOB's Building Permits layers inside the DC Health Petworth boundary. Sales use OTR's "Petworth" assessment neighborhood, which is close to but not identical to that boundary.
What's mocked
The sponsor card ([Agent name], [Brokerage], the [AN] initials and the contact button) is a placeholder, not a real agent. Rent ranges from the original idea are left out because there is no open rent source. Only each parcel's most recent sale is in the tax extract, so the earlier sale of a home that resold within 24 months is missing, which slightly undercounts the older quarters.
To build it for real
Pull full sale history from Tax System Property Sales for exact quarterly counts; add condo $/sq ft from CAMA Condominium; add a rent snapshot only from a licensed or open source; build the sponsor admin (headshot, link, 12-month term) and a one-page sponsor pitch with traffic numbers.
