Zoning Commission writes and changes the rules and the map: rezonings, text changes and large Planned Unit Developments.

About this demo

The idea
Every BZA and Zoning Commission case touching the neighborhood gets one plain-English entry: what's requested, its status, which ANC it's in, and where to read the filings and testify.
What's real here
Case numbers, filing dates, status, addresses, ANC/SMD, official descriptions and the specific relief requested come from the Office of Zoning's "Zoning Cases" layer, queried live on 2026-10-31 inside the DC Health planning-neighborhood boundaries for Tenleytown (which includes Friendship Heights) and Petworth (59 cases). Case-name and owner fields, which usually carry the applicant's name, are never requested.
What's mocked
Only the sponsor slot. The "In plain English" notes are our generic glosses of the relief type and the rule cited, written once and matched by keyword; they are not legal advice and are not specific to each case. Hearing dates and ANC votes are not shown because this dataset doesn't carry them.
To build it for real
Pull hearing dates from the Office of Zoning's public hearing calendar, link each ANC's filed resolution from IZIS exhibits, add a small map per case, send a weekly email when a case is filed or decided, and have a human review the gloss on big Zoning Commission cases.
