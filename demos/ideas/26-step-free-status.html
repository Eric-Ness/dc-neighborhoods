About this demo

The idea
A small status block on each neighborhood site for wheelchair users, parents with strollers, travelers with luggage and older residents: is the elevator working, since when has it been out, and where to go instead. Free push or email alerts, 90-day history.
What's real here
Station locations and lines come from DC GIS's Metro Stations layer, pulled 2026-10-31. From them the page computes each plan-B station (nearest other station) and which station is nearer to Adams Morgan, using the neighborhood boundary from DC GIS. elevatoruptime.com was confirmed to exist and publish per-station uptime by a web search on 2026-10-31.
What's mocked
Every elevator status, outage, estimated return, and uptime figure is sample data, including the two outages shown (Tenleytown–AU and L'Enfant Plaza). They are not real outages. Live status needs a WMATA API key we don't have. The alert sign-up, ntfy topic and embed snippet are mockups.
To build it for real
Register a WMATA developer key. A Cloudflare Worker calls the ElevatorIncidents endpoint (Incidents.svc/json/ElevatorIncidents) every 5 minutes, filters to these six stations by station code, and writes each change to D1. That table is the outage history, so the 90-day chart builds itself, and each new or cleared outage posts to the station's ntfy topic and email list. Serve every visitor from the cached JSON, as WMATA's terms require.
