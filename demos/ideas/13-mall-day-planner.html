About this demo

The idea
A one-page morning-of dashboard for visitors coming through L'Enfant Plaza: Mall closures and events, museum hours, weather tips, the free SW Shuttle, walking times and elevator status for strollers, printable for hotel front desks.
What's real here
Live at build time: the National Weather Service forecast for the station's grid point and Capital Bikeshare dock counts within 700 m. Verified on the operators' pages: the SW Shuttle's hours and stops (wharfdc.com), the station's entrances and elevators (wmata.com), and the Air and Space Museum's hours (si.edu, read from search results because si.edu blocked our direct fetch; confirm before relying on it).
What's mocked
The NPS alert and permitted-event lines are placeholders, and elevator status is sample (no NPS or WMATA keys yet). Other museum hours are not shown at all, only "Check hours" links. Walk times are map-distance estimates (×1.3, 80 m/min) from approximate coordinates.
To build it for real
Register free NPS and WMATA keys. Run a 6 a.m. Worker cron that pulls NPS alerts and events for the Mall parks, NWS forecast and heat index, WMATA ElevatorIncidents, and the shuttle page, then re-renders this static page. Museum hours have no API, so scrape each si.edu museum page with a check that fails loudly when the layout changes, and fall back to "Check hours" links. Time the walks on foot once. Put ads and hotel sponsorship only on the lenfantplaza.net copy.
