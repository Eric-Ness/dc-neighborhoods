About this demo

The idea
A generated page for every block face that combines permits, 311, crashes, trees, liquor licenses, school boundaries and the SMD into one shareable 'know your block' card.
What's real here
Everything was pulled from Open Data DC on 2026-10-31: DDOT Roadway Block (street attributes and geometry), Building Permits (last 12 months, matched by address on the block), 311 City Service Requests (server-side counts by type, so house numbers never leave DC's server), Crashes in DC (counts within 150 m of the block line), Urban Forestry Street Trees (within 15 m), ABCA licensee locations (within 250 m of mid-block; that layer is a periodic snapshot), DCPS attendance zones and the 2023 SMD layer (point lookup at mid-block).
What's mocked
Nothing in the data. Block choice (picked for having both permits and 311 activity) and the layout are design decisions. Lead-pipe status is a link to DC Water, not ingested. Owner and applicant names, 311 details and crash report numbers are never requested; crashes are shown only as counts.
To build it for real
Build one joined dataset per neighborhood in the nightly ingest, emit a static Astro page per Roadway Block segment, add 'nearby blocks' links and sales history, and keep pages substantive enough to avoid looking like an SEO farm.
