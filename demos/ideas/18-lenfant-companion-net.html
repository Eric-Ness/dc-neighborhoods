About this demo

The idea
The commercial companion on lenfantplaza.net: a fast mobile page with arrival boards by platform, an outage strip, a walking-time grid, food open now, and paid hotel-map and tour placements for the tourists and conference-goers passing through. The ad-free how-to-transfer guide lives on the .org.
What's real here
Capital Bikeshare dock counts near the station were pulled live from the public GBFS feed at build time. Platform names (upper-level platforms to Branch Ave/Huntington and Greenbelt/Mt Vernon Sq; lower-level Blue/Orange/Silver) and the four-elevator count follow WMATA's station page. Walk times are map-distance estimates.
What's mocked
Arrival boards, car counts and the outage strip are sample data (no WMATA key). Every hotel, tour, ticket and ad box is an unfilled [Affiliate placement] with no real business named. Food spots use placeholder names ("Sample Café"). Exit-per-destination is unverified.
To build it for real
Register a WMATA key and serve predictions and ElevatorIncidents from one cached Worker; confirm WMATA's terms allow display on an ad-supported page and add the required non-endorsement line. Apply to Stay22 and a tour affiliate (GetYourGuide or Viator) and drop their widgets into the marked boxes. Pull food hours from a places source with a commercial license, or sell those slots to local spots. Walk the station to fill the exit-per-destination column.
