About this demo

The idea
A once-a-year, press-ready data report per neighborhood (permits, homes, businesses, 311, safety, representation) published as a microsite and PDF. A bank, developer or brokerage pays as title sponsor; a CSV data pack is sold to brokers and retailers.
What's real here
Every number. This run pulled 24 months of building permits inside the DC Health Petworth boundary (2,996 permits), 12 monthly 311 counts plus counts by type, MPD crime counts by offense for two 12-month windows, 370 OTR market sales, business-license counts for ANC 4C and 4D, and the vacant/blighted address count. The 30-day permit count and the commissioner list come from the nightly ingest file generated 2026-10-31.
What's mocked
The title sponsor and data-pack slots are placeholders. Business licenses use ANC 4C and 4D, which are close to but not the same as the Petworth boundary, and home sales use OTR's Petworth assessment neighborhood. Metro ridership and foot-traffic chapters from the original idea are left out (WMATA ridership needs a separate download).
To build it for real
Freeze a dated snapshot each October so year-over-year numbers are reproducible; add a methodology appendix and printable PDF; add WMATA station ridership; pitch three title sponsors per neighborhood with this demo; offer the CSV pack via a simple checkout.
Independent project; not affiliated with DC Government, any ANC, BID or property owner.
