Comparables: same house type, a recorded market sale in the last 18 months, then ranked by same OTR sub-area, nearest tax square, closest building size and most recent sale. "Indicated value" is your building area × the comparables' median price per square foot (or their median price when size is missing). Sale prices lag deeds by a few weeks, and assessments value the property as of January 1, so small gaps are normal. The pre-filled address is an example picked automatically from public tax records, not a judgment about that home.

About this demo

The idea
A monthly list of recorded home sales plus a free tool that compares a homeowner's assessment with nearby comparable sales, timed to the appeal season. A single local real-estate or mortgage sponsor pays for the page; the tool is never gated.
What's real here
Everything above. This run pulled 5,161 Petworth houses (row, semi-detached, detached) from OTR's Integrated Tax System Public Extract: address, sub-area, tax square, current assessment, last recorded sale and OTR's sale code. Building area and bedrooms come from CAMA Residential, joined by SSL. Sales shown are those OTR codes as "Market Sale" at $150,000 or more. Owner names exist in the tax data and were never requested.
What's mocked
The sponsor card is a placeholder. Comparables use tax-square numbers as a stand-in for distance because the tax extract has no coordinates. Only each parcel's most recent sale is in the extract, so a home sold twice in 18 months counts once. Condos are left out of this demo.
To build it for real
Add parcel coordinates (Address Points or tax lots) for true distance-based comps and a map; add condos via CAMA Condominium; refresh nightly with the existing ingest; send an appeal-season reminder when notices mail; have a local appraiser review the comp logic and wording.
