What the lessee gets

  • The exact-match .org for the neighborhood name, the one people guess first.
  • Redirects or a labeled banner, live within 48 hours of signing.
  • Forwarding email addresses at the domain (for example events@), pointing to your inbox.
  • A monthly one-paragraph report: visits, top pages, clicks to your site, once analytics are running. Aggregate numbers only.
  • The owner keeps the registration, renewals, DNS and security. You never pay registrar fees or worry about expiry.

Editorial independence: the rules that come with the lease

  1. The data stays independent. Permits, 311, zoning and other public-record sections are produced by an automated pipeline from city data. The lessee can't edit, remove, delay or reorder them.
  2. Your content is labeled as yours. Anything the lessee supplies appears under "From [Lessee name]", visually separate from the data.
  3. No paywall on civic data. Public records stay free to read.
  4. No resident data changes hands. No email lists, no tracking pixels for the lessee, no personal data. Aggregate traffic only.
  5. Corrections follow the evidence. Factual errors are fixed when shown, whoever reports them. Complaints about accurate public records are not corrections.
  6. No official airs. The site never implies it is run by DC Government, an ANC or the lessee. Every page carries "friendshipheights.org is leased to [Lessee name]; data sections are independent."
  7. Conflict rule. We won't lease to a political campaign, or to a party with an open case before the BZA or Zoning Commission in the neighborhood, while that case is open.
  8. Clean exit. When the lease ends, the domain reverts and the lessee's content is removed within 7 days. Nothing is held hostage on either side.

About this demo

The idea
Instead of running a business on the domain, rent the domain itself: a one- to three-year lease, or a build-and-maintain deal, to the party that values the name most, with written rules that keep the civic data independent.
What's real here
The three data figures (permits, 311 requests, Single Member Districts) are read from the project's nightly ingest output for the Tenleytown planning area, generated Oct 31, 2026. That ingest pulls Open Data DC; this generator makes no API calls of its own.
What's mocked
Everything commercial: prices, terms, discounts, the banner preview, the owner and lessee placeholders. Lessee types are generic categories; no real organization is named or has shown interest. There are no traffic figures because none have been measured.
To build it for real
Point the domain at a live Friendship Heights page for a few months to collect real traffic, draft the lease with a lawyer (the independence rules become contract clauses), then send this page to two or three candidates by category, one at a time.
