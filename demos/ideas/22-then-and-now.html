About this demo

The idea
Drag-to-compare sliders that pair a public-domain historic photo with a new photo shot from the same spot, organized by street. Then-and-now pairs are among the most shared local-history posts, and the owner's walks become the content pipeline.
What's real here
Both historic photos are real Library of Congress items, fetched and embedded on 2026-10-31 only after each live record said "No known restrictions on publication". The slider works with mouse, touch and keyboard.
What's mocked
The "now" side of every pair is a placeholder panel, because the matching photos haven't been taken yet, so the page is badged sample. The camera position for each historic photo is not yet confirmed. The "next pairs" list and the print offer are illustrative.
To build it for real
Search LOC (National Photo Company, Harris & Ewing, FSA/OWI, HABS) and DDOT's pre-1973 photo library for 15–30 public-domain views per neighborhood, find each camera position, reshoot at the same focal length and season, then publish pairs on a pin map with prints for sale.
Independent project; not affiliated with DC Government, any ANC, BID or property owner. Sources: Library of Congress Prints & Photographs Division (National Photo Company and FSA/OWI rights statements). Generated 2026-10-31 17:35 UTC.
