About this demo

The idea
A weekly (or daily) feed of new building permits, and later ABCA license applications, sold to contractors, architects, solar and roofing installers, restaurant suppliers and commercial brokers.
What's real here
All 661 rows are DOB building permits issued citywide since Oct 24, pulled from Open Data DC's 'Building Permits in 2026' layer on 2026-10-31. The newest issue date in the layer was 2026-10-28; DOB posts with a lag of a few days. Type groups, trade tags and fee bands are computed by the generator from DOB's own fields (subtype, description of work, fees paid). Filtering runs in your browser over that snapshot.
What's mocked
Plans, prices, the CSV and saved-filter buttons are mockups. ABCA license-application leads are not included yet (they come from weekly PDFs). Owner and applicant names are never shown, even though lead buyers would want them: the generator does not request those fields, and the product would not sell them. Addresses are cut to the hundred block in this preview; fees are shown as bands, not exact amounts. Fees paid are a rough proxy for job size, not project value.
To build it for real
Run the same query nightly, keep a 90-day archive, add ABCA weekly-issuance parsing, deliver saved filters by email and webhook, take payments through Stripe, and check DOB and ABCA terms before any outreach use.
