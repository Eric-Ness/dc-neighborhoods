About this demo

The idea
Historical odds that each Capital Bikeshare dock in the neighborhood has a bike, or an open slot, at a given hour and weekday, with a plain-English tip per station and a monthly rebalancing report card.
What's real here
The "Right now" table: one live snapshot of Capital Bikeshare's public GBFS feed pulled 2026-10-31 (2026-10-31 16:50 UTC) for 26 stations inside or within 250 m of the Petworth and Tenleytown (which includes Friendship Heights) neighborhood boundaries from DC GIS. Station names, bikes, e-bikes and open docks are as reported by the feed.
What's mocked
The hour-by-weekday odds, the per-station tips and the rebalancing report card are sample figures, generated from a simple made-up pattern (homes drain in the morning, docks by Metro fill). We have one snapshot, not the weeks of history real odds need. The sponsor is a placeholder.
To build it for real
A Cloudflare Worker cron reads station_status every 5 minutes (288 snapshots a day; about 7,488 rows a day for these 26 stations) into D1, with a nightly rollup to hour-by-weekday counts so the table stays small. After four to six weeks, each cell becomes the share of snapshots with zero bikes (or zero docks) in that hour. Capital Bikeshare's monthly trip-history files could backfill demand patterns. Check the GBFS license in system_information before launch.
