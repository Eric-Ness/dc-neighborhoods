The house's story

4920 43rd Place NW was permitted in 1935. The original permit (No. 184452, issued September 14, 1935) names Dillon & Abel as architect and E. M. Willis as builder, and estimates the cost at $6,500. It describes a detached dwelling of brick & tile, 6 rooms, on a brick & tile foundation under a slate pitch roof, heated by hot water. About this demo

The idea
A permanent page for every pre-1950 building in the neighborhood: who designed and built it, what it cost, what it was made of, its block's construction history, and today's permits. Pages like this rank for address searches and need almost no upkeep.
What's real here
Everything on the page was queried by the generator on 2026-10-31: the HistoryQuest permit record and nearby footprints, citywide architect and builder counts, the historic-district and landmark check, and DOB building permits at the address. The story paragraph is filled in from those fields by a template.
What's mocked
The sponsor slot and the "Suggest an edit" button. The page links to the Sanborn collection rather than embedding a cropped, georeferenced sheet of this exact block, which would need manual georeferencing.
To build it for real
Generate one page per HistoryQuest address in each neighborhood (Tenleytown alone has about 4,000 records with architect, builder, cost and material). Match permits by normalized address. Add Sanborn sheet crops once sheets are georeferenced (Allmaps or QGIS), a correction queue, and block pages that link to each house.
Independent project; not affiliated with DC Government, any ANC, BID or property owner.
