About this demo

The idea
An automatic weekly page (plus a Monday email) listing every notable permit, zoning case and street-occupancy permit inside the neighborhood, in plain English, so neighbors know what's going on at that house before they have to ask.
What's real here
All items were pulled from Open Data DC on 2026-10-31: Building Permits (DOB yearly layer), Zoning Cases, DDOT TOPS Occupancy Permits, and 311 City Service Requests (server-side counts only), clipped to the DC Health planning area for Tenleytown (which includes Friendship Heights). Weeks run Monday to Sunday, Eastern time. Electrical, plumbing, minor and home-business permits are rolled up into the 'Also this week' line. This differs from the existing digest demo, which is a 30-day summary: this page is a running weekly log with one map per week.
What's mocked
The one-line summaries are a rule-based cleanup of DOB's work description, standing in for the cached LLM summaries the idea proposes. Liquor-license actions are left out: the ABCA layer on Open Data DC is a periodic snapshot of current licenses, and placard notices are PDFs that are not parsed yet. The subscribe form and the sponsor line are mockups. Owner and applicant names, zoning case names and 311 details are never requested. DDOT permit locations are shown to the hundred block.
To build it for real
Store each night's items keyed by permit or case number so weeks freeze once they end, add per-street archive pages, parse ABCA placard PDFs, cache an LLM summary per permit, and send the Monday email through Buttondown or Cloudflare Email.
Independent project; not affiliated with DC Government, any ANC, BID or property owner.
