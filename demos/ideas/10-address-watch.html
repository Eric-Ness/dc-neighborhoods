About this demo

The idea
Enter your address and get an email the day a permit, curb reservation, 311 request or (opt-in) crime report appears within a chosen radius. One block is free; a few dollars a month covers more addresses, wider radii and instant delivery.
What's real here
Every alert was pulled from Open Data DC on 2026-10-31 with a 500 ft circle around DC's Intersection Points record for Wisconsin Avenue NW & Albemarle Street NW: Building Permits (DOB), DDOT TOPS Occupancy Permits, 311 City Service Requests (type, date, status and hundred block only), Crime Incidents (server-side counts by offense, never incidents) and ABCA licensee locations (a periodic snapshot). Street lines are DDOT's Roadway Block layer.
What's mocked
The subscriber is a sample: a made-up account watching a public intersection, not anyone's home. The delivery settings, plan names and prices are proposals. No ABCA notices are shown because placard PDFs are not parsed yet. Owner and applicant names, 311 details and individual crime incidents are never requested.
To build it for real
Store watched points (encrypted) in Cloudflare D1, run each night's new records through a point-in-radius check, send digests with Cloudflare Email Workers, take payments with Stripe, and add ABCA placard parsing.
