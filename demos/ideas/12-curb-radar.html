About this demo

The idea
A daily map and alert feed of every DDOT public-space permit on the neighborhood's streets: who has reserved the curb, where a crane is going up, and which block loses parking next week.
What's real here
Every permit, date, curb length and map dot comes from DDOT's TOPS layers on Open Data DC, pulled 2026-10-31: Occupancy Permits (via DDOT TOPs) and Construction Permits (via DDOT TOPs), both found through the Open Data DC search API. Only permits with status Issued or Approved whose dates overlap today–+7 days are shown. The freshness panel is computed from the live layer (max issue and update dates, per-day counts). Street lines are DDOT's Roadway Block layer.
What's mocked
The 'Follow this street' buttons and the alert form are non-working mockups; nothing is sent or stored. Type groupings and colors are my own. Permit holders' names (owner, applicant, permittee, contractor) are in the source data but are never requested or shown.
To build it for real
Run the same two queries in the nightly ingest, keep yesterday's snapshot to detect newly approved permits, send per-street alerts with Cloudflare Email Workers, and add an RSS feed per street. Watch the layer's update date and hide the page if it goes stale.
