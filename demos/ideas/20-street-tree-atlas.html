About this demo

The idea
Every public street tree in the neighborhood, with species, size and health, plus a finder for empty tree boxes where residents can request a free DDOT planting.
What's real here
All counts, the species chart, the biggest-trees table, the map and the empty-box list come from DDOT's Urban Forestry Street Trees layer, queried live inside the Petworth boundary on 2026-10-31 (4,389 records). The empty-box finding is from the layer's own tree-box status field (TBOX_STAT).
What's mocked
Only the sponsor slot. Our reading of the status values (Open = empty and plantable, Proposed = scheduled, Conflict = blocked) is from the values and their notes, not from a published DDOT data dictionary, so treat it as our interpretation. Arborist notes are not shown.
To build it for real
Add the Street Tree Archive to show plantings and removals per year since 2014, a species filter on the map, a per-street "biggest oak" page, and a seasonal "what's blooming" page from species bloom months. Refresh monthly.
