About this demo

The idea
A phone-first guide to the most confusing transfer in the system: pick your train and your connection, get plain steps, the next trains on the connecting platform and the step-free route if an elevator is out. A second tab says what's above each street exit.
What's real here
Capital Bikeshare dock counts (Bikes tab) were pulled live from the public GBFS feed when this page was built (5 docks within 700 m). The station's layout is from WMATA's own pages: Green/Yellow on upper-level side platforms (one to Branch Ave/Huntington, one to Greenbelt/Mt Vernon Sq), Orange/Blue/Silver on a lower-level center platform, three street entrances (7th St/Maryland Ave, 9th & D St, 7th & D St), four elevators, and the end-of-platform escalator up to a mezzanine that crosses over the Green/Yellow tracks. Walk times to places are straight-line estimates (×1.3, 80 m/min).
What's mocked
Next-train minutes and elevator status are sample values (no WMATA API key); one elevator is shown out on purpose so you can see the step-free alert. Everything in [brackets] (platform ends, escalator banks, exit letters, transfer walk times, short links) is unverified and needs an on-site walk.
To build it for real
Register a free WMATA key and run one Cloudflare Worker on a 30-second cron that calls Rail Predictions for L'Enfant's two station codes (upper and lower level) and ElevatorIncidents, caches both in KV, and feeds this page; visitors never hit WMATA directly. Walk the station once with a phone timer to fill every bracket, photograph the exit letters, and stamp a visible "last verified" date. Add a "not affiliated with WMATA" line and no Metro marks, per WMATA's data terms.
