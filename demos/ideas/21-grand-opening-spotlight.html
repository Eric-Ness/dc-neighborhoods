About this demo

The idea
An automatically built list of new businesses from license and occupancy records, with a $49–149 paid "Featured" spotlight for owners who want to announce their opening. Items are labeled by the record that exists ("license issued"), never "opened".
What's real here
Every listing. This run pulled 216 Basic Business Licenses first issued in the last 60 days in these ANCs (Adams Morgan: ANC 1C; Petworth: ANC 4C, 4D; Tenleytown & Friendship Heights: ANC 3E, 3/4G; SW Waterfront: ANC 6D) and kept 16 storefront-type businesses (food, retail and services). 53 were dropped as likely home-based: an apartment address, an address DC's address registry marks as residential, or a sole proprietor with no trade name (whose legal name is a person's name and is never shown). It also pulled 21 non-residential certificates of occupancy inside the same ANC boundaries and the newest active ABCA liquor licenses there.
What's mocked
The Featured card and the "Claim this listing" links are placeholders. The liquor-license section is from ABCA's latest open snapshot, not the last 60 days, because that dataset has no issue dates. Matching by ANC is approximate at neighborhood edges, and grouping licenses into one business by address and name is automatic.
To build it for real
Track ABCA placard notices and hearing agendas for true liquor-license dates; add DC Health food inspections when a bulk feed exists; review items weekly before the email goes out; build the claim form and a checkout for spotlights; match by neighborhood polygon once license coordinates are published at full precision (they are rounded to whole degrees today).
