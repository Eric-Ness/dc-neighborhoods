About this demo

The idea
A weekly column and email that mines the public-domain Evening Star for each neighborhood, 100 years back. The source never runs dry: every January another year of the paper enters the public domain.
What's real here
The searches, page counts and every clipping were pulled from loc.gov on 2026-10-31: Evening Star pages dated October 31 – November 6, 1926 that match each neighborhood's search terms. Clippings are the Library's own OCR text around the most legible mention on each page, with only hyphen and symbol clean-up, and each links to its scanned page.
What's mocked
The sign-up box and sponsor slot. In the product an editor would add a one-line plain-English summary and, where needed, historical context to each item. This demo shows raw clippings and simply holds back items that mention race, covenants or deed restrictions.
To build it for real
Run weekly in GitHub Actions. Widen each neighborhood's search to more period and street names (Columbia Road, Wisconsin Avenue with Tenley, Georgia Avenue). Have Claude rank items and draft summaries for a 15-minute human review, crop the article image from the page (the LOC word-coordinates service gives the box), then publish to the site and the newsletter.
