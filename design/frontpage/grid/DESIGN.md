# Instrument

**Idea:** the front page as a calm civic instrument: a twelve-column grid, black type on white, and one vermilion used only to say "this is the thing to look at".

**Layout.** Twelve sections, each opened by a 2px rule, an index number and a flush-left heading, so the page reads like a table of contents you can scan in ten seconds. News (7 columns) and the next 14 days of events (4 columns) share the first screen. Then a one-sentence condition with a rising/easing/steady tally, four measures, the permit map beside its numbered project list, and paired half-width modules. No cards or shadows; alignment, hairlines and white space do the work. On a phone everything stacks to one column, the measures become a 2×2 grid, and map marks scale up so they stay tappable.

**Type and color.** Archivo, one family, tabular lining figures throughout so numbers line up in columns. Big numerals for the measures; small gray captions give the period and basis. Ink #111, gray #5e5e5e (6.5:1), vermilion #c43a12 (5.3:1) for today's date, bigger projects and their map marks only. Crime uses the same neutral ink bars as everything else.

**Dropped or demoted.** Commissioner names (the brief bars person names from data): SMD codes plus a link to each ANC site. Architect names in the 1926 block story, for the same reason. Jobs and liquor licenses: not front-page news. Clips are picked by an OCR-noise score, so the most readable ones show. The sponsor slot is gone.
