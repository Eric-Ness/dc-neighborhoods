Rodent complaints climb as 311 calls fall

Petworth, Oct. 31 — Rodent complaints rose to 113 in the past 30 days, up from 84, even as total 311 requests in Petworth fell from 1,346 to 1,160.

The biggest drop was in yard-waste pickups, down to 132 from 167. Heating-bill help (LIHEAP) requests appeared this period: 35, up from none.
