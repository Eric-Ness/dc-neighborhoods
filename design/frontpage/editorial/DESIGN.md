# Broadsheet

**Idea:** Petworth's front page as a small-city newspaper that reports the night's data, not a dashboard.

**Layout.** A nameplate with a double rule and a dateline row. Above the fold sit three columns. The widest holds the newest headline as the lead, two seconds and the briefs. Headlines older than 30 days go under "Earlier this year", so stale news doesn't pass for new. A narrow column holds "Coming up". "Tonight's reading" is a short data story: a generated headline, a dateline lede, a rising/easing/steady tally strip and the largest moves. Next comes a ruled "By the numbers" band of four measures, each with a then/now bar. "What's changing" sets the permit map beside numbered projects. Homes, Streets and Then share three columns. ANC and email close the page. On a phone it all runs as one column in reading order, and the map markers scale up.

**Type and color.** Newsreader, a news serif, sets headlines and body text. Condensed Archivo sets kickers, meta lines and figures. The page is white with near-black ink and one brick-red accent. Crime counts use the same neutral ink as everything else. Hairline rules replace cards.

**Dropped or demoted.** The privacy rule bars person names, so commissioner and architect names are gone and the page links to the ANC sites. Jobs, liquor licenses, 311 service times and RSS become links. The data has no prior-period permit count, so the page shows the permit mix and says so.
