# Signal

**Idea:** Petworth gets its own wordmark and route line: a brick-red line between two stations, Grant Circle and Sherman Circle. Every section is a stop on that line.

**Layout.** The masthead is the wordmark, set huge. Under it, a three-stop "today" line (next event, newest headline, the 311 change) gives the phone check-in in one screen. News leads in magazine serif, beside a dark "Coming up" departure board with amber dates. Next, a gray band holds the condition sentence, the rising/easing/steady tally and four measures. Then come the permit map (sticky on desktop) with numbered projects, your block (curb permits, top 311), homes, trees beside 1926, the ANCs, and a full-bleed brick email band.

**Type.** Archivo's width axis carries the brand: wide and heavy for the wordmark and numbers, condensed for section heads. Source Serif 4 sets headlines and lead sentences.

**Color.** Ink #0E1D25 on white, with brick #B0351B (Petworth's rowhouses) as the brand color. Amber marks highlights, green means trees only. Rising and easing are ink and gray, never red or green. Crime is plain ink, inside a disclosure. All text passes 4.5:1. Rules do the structuring, with no cards, shadows or rounded boxes.

**Dropped or demoted.** Commissioner and architect names are out (privacy), and so are the jobs and licenses lists. Older news shrinks to two items on phones, and the curb list shows 7 permits with a link to the rest. Permits have no prior period, and the page says so.
