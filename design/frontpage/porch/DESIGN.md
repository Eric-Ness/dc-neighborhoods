# Porch

**Idea:** the neighborhood's front porch board: the page you check each morning, organized as "This week in Petworth": today, coming up, then what changed, then your block.

**Layout.** A big dated headline with a one-sentence summary written from the data (markets today and tomorrow, stories this week, curb reservations starting). Below it, a two-column board: a pale "porch-ceiling blue" panel for today and tomorrow, with the next two weeks under it, and news on the right (this week's stories large, older ones in a dated list). Then a tinted band for "What changed this month": the auto-written 311 sentence, a rising/easing/steady tally, and four measures. Then "On your block": a numbered permit map tied to the bigger-projects list, curb reservations starting this week, and permits by kind. Homes, trees and top 311 requests share one row; then "This week in 1926"; the footer holds the email signup and the commissions. On a phone it's one column in reading order: today, news, coming up.

**Type & color.** Bricolage Grotesque for headlines, labels and numbers; Newsreader for news headlines and the written sentences, so writing reads as writing. White page, deep porch green, haint blue, brick red for dates and markers, and a porch-light amber used only for "today" and focus rings. Crime gets neutral styling.

**Demoted/dropped.** Commissioner names (privacy; districts and ANC links stay), exact sale addresses (hundred-block only), liquor-license cancellations (no dates in data), jobs (two listings, both outside Petworth).
