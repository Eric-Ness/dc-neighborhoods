No. 05

History

400 block of Delafield Place NW

The Cafritz Construction Co. built all 62 rowhouses in 1926 on four permits; architects H. H. Warwick (52) and D. W. Moore (10). One permit covered 21 houses for $105,000.

100 years ago this week: October 31 – November 6, 1926, Evening Star (Library of Congress)
