This week · 311 requests

Rodent complaints rose to 118, up from 84 the month before

Calls to DC Health’s rodent and vector control line went up by 34 in the past 30 days, while total 311 requests in Petworth fell from 1,336 to 1,168.

From DC 311 open data · last 30 days vs. the 30 before

Rodent and vector control was the third most common 311 request in Petworth over the past 30 days, with 118 requests. Only bulk collection (210) and scheduled yard waste (131) drew more.

It was one of the few request types to go up. Overall, residents filed 168 fewer requests than in the previous 30 days. Illegal dumping reports fell from 95 to 68, and yard-waste pickups from 166 to 131.
